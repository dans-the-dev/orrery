# Orrery visual directions

**Chosen 2026-11-06 by the owner: 03 Current Limit.** Its `STYLE.md` and `tokens.css` are the frontend guide for
Orrery. Needlehouse and Red Seal stay as references. Revision 2 of Needlehouse and Red Seal was done by Codex;
Current Limit revision 2 was finished by the planning session after Codex hit its usage limit; the two other
directions' `STYLE.md` files still describe revision 1 layouts.

Open [the gallery](index.html) to compare the three static directions. Each includes a landing page, an operator policy screen, a public signer page, design tokens, a style guide, and desktop and mobile previews.

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
